> Generated by storefront-audit-harness 0.1.0 for `https://gingerpeople.com/` at 2026-06-16T04:10:00Z.

## Executive summary

The single most expensive issue at gingerpeople.com right now is that the storefront is not reachable by automated clients at all. Every surface the harness requested — homepage, /cart, robots.txt and sitemap.xml — returned an HTTP 403 'Just a moment...' Cloudflare bot-management challenge instead of page content. That means this audit could not observe any product, pricing, or merchandising detail; what follows is an access-and-infrastructure audit, and a content-level CRO audit requires an unblocked (real-browser) crawl.

This is not a cosmetic finding. An interactive bot challenge that returns 403 to non-JavaScript clients blocks search-engine and social-preview crawlers (hurting organic discovery and link previews), blocks the emerging class of AI shopping and comparison agents (hurting agentic-commerce demand), blocks accessibility scanners and link/uptime monitors, and adds a pre-paint interstitial that can raise bounce for real human visitors on slow or privacy-restricted browsers. Returning, high-intent sessions arriving via email, SMS, retargeting, or affiliate deep links to /cart hit the same wall — a silent loss of the most valuable traffic.

The experiments below span all five pillars, but they are deliberately framed around the access barrier and its measurable revenue consequences, with honestly modest confidence because no page content was observable. The priority is to make verified crawlers, agents, and returning human sessions pass through protection cleanly; once the store is observable again, re-run the harness to produce the content-level Conversion/AOV/Retention experiments this brand likely warrants.
